✦ Based on a detailed comparison between the FAI Sporting Code: Rules and Regulations for Air Navigation Race (2022 Edition) provided in the PDF and the FAI ANR 2022 Scorecard
  implementation, here is the non-compliance report.


  1. Finish Point (FP) Extended Gate Penalty
   * Rule (A.1.2.12 & A.8.1.5): The rules state that both the Start Point (SP) and Finish Point (FP) have an extended gate line of 0.3 NM on either side. Crossing this line
     opposite to the direction of flight (signifying circling or backtracking) is penalized. Under A.8.1.5, "Circling or backtracking" carries a 200-point penalty.
   * Implementation: The scorecard sets bad_crossing_extended_gate_penalty to 0 for the FINISHPOINT.
   * Status: Non-compliant. The penalty should be 200 points to match the SP and the general backtracking rule.


  2. Corridor Excursion Grace Time
   * Rule (A.1.2.10): "All corridors must be passed by the Competitor inside." The regulations do not explicitly grant a grace period for being outside the competition corridor.
     (Note: Rule A.8.1.6 grants a 5-second grace for Restricted Areas, but the corridor itself is not defined as such).
   * Implementation: The scorecard includes a corridor_grace_time of 5 seconds.
   * Status: Potentially Non-compliant (Oversight). By allowing 5 seconds of flight outside the corridor before penalties accrue, the implementation is more lenient than the
     "must be inside" requirement of the Sporting Code.


  3. Distance-Based Backtracking Grace
   * Rule (A.8.1.5): Backtracking is strictly defined as a turn of more than 90 degrees from the track. No distance-based exemptions are mentioned in the Sporting Code.
   * Implementation: The scorecard utilizes backtracking_after_gate_grace_period_nm and backtracking_before_gate_grace_period_nm set to 0.5 NM.
   * Status: Non-compliant. The current implementation allows a pilot to make a 180-degree turn (e.g., to kill time before a gate) as long as they are within 0.5 NM of the
     waypoint. The FAI rules penalize any turn >90 degrees "each time" regardless of the proximity to a gate.


  4. Corridor Maximum Penalty
   * Rule (A.8.1.5): While the rules are silent on a specific "per-second" corridor penalty in the provided table, if excursions are treated as "Other deviations from track,"
     the maximum penalty is 400 points.
   * Implementation: corridor_maximum_penalty is set to 0 (which typically denotes "uncapped" in this engine).
   * Status: Discrepancy. If the intent is to cap corridor penalties per task/leg to prevent a single error from resulting in thousands of points, the scorecard should reflect a
     maximum (e.g., 400).

  ---


  Summary of Compliant Areas
   * Timing Penalties (A.8.1.3): The 2-second grace period, 3 points per second rate, 200-point maximum, and 200-point missed gate penalty all match exactly.
   * Starting Point (SP) Width (A.1.2.12): The extended gate width of 0.6 NM (0.3 NM each side) matches the regulation.
   * Take-off Timing (A.8.1.2): The 60-second grace window and the 200-point penalty for being outside the slot are compliant.
   * Minimum Altitude (A.8.1.4): The 500-point penalty for flying below minimum altitude matches the rule.
   * Backtracking Points (A.8.1.5): The 200-point penalty per occurrence and 400-point maximum match the rule (excluding the NM grace issue mentioned above).
