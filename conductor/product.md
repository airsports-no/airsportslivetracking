# Product Definition: Air Sports Live Tracking (ASLT)

## 1. Vision

Air Sports Live Tracking (ASLT) is an online (live) scoring platform for aircraft competitions. It aims to provide a comprehensive solution for pilots, organizers, and spectators to engage with air sports events in real-time.

## 2. Target Users

ASLT serves a diverse set of users, each with specific needs:

*   **Pilots/Contestants:** Participate in competitions, track their flights live, and view their scores and rankings.
*   **Competition Organizers/Administrators:** Create, manage, and score competitions. They handle everything from defining tasks and rules to managing contestant entries.
*   **Spectators/Public:** Follow competitions in real-time on a live map, view leaderboards, and engage with the sport.
*   **Third-Party Integrators:** Connect external tools and simulators (like Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020) to the platform for training or virtual competitions.

## 3. Core Features

To meet the needs of its users, ASLT focuses on the following core features:

*   **Live Tracking:** Real-time visualization of aircraft positions on a map, providing an engaging experience for all user groups.
*   **Automated Scoring:** A powerful and flexible scoring engine that automatically calculates results based on various competition rules and task types (e.g., precision flying, air navigation races).
*   **User & Authentication Management:** Secure user registration, login, and profile management for pilots and administrators.
*   **Contest & Task Management:** A comprehensive toolset for organizers to create new contests, define navigation tasks, manage contestant registrations, and oversee the entire competition lifecycle.
