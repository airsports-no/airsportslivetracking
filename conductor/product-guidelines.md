# Product Guidelines: Air Sports Live Tracking (ASLT)

## 1. Prose Style & Tone

All user-facing communication, including in-app text, documentation, and marketing materials, should adhere to the following principles:

*   **Professional yet Approachable:** The tone should be authoritative and trustworthy, reflecting the precision required in aviation. However, it must also be welcoming and accessible to a broad audience, from seasoned pilots to first-time spectators. Avoid overly technical jargon where possible, or provide clear explanations when it's necessary.
*   **Clarity and Conciseness:** Prioritize clear and direct communication. Get to the point quickly and efficiently, ensuring that users can easily understand and act upon the information provided.
*   **Encouraging and Positive:** Foster a sense of community and excitement around air sports. The language should be encouraging to contestants and engaging for spectators.

## 2. Visual Identity

The visual identity of ASLT should be a unique blend of modern design, dynamic energy, and classic aviation heritage.

*   **Design Philosophy:** The overall design should be **modern, clean, and dynamic**. It should feel professional and data-driven, with a strong focus on usability and clear information hierarchy.
*   **Color Palette:** The primary color palette should be modern and bold, but can incorporate **tones from classic aviation themes**. This could mean using a base of clean whites and dark grays, accented with a dynamic, bold color (like a vibrant blue or orange) and potentially some more subdued, classic aviation-inspired secondary colors.
*   **Typography:** Fonts should be clean, legible, and modern. A sans-serif font family is recommended for both headings and body text to ensure readability on digital screens.
*   **Imagery:** While the overall theme is modern, imagery and iconography can give a nod to **classic aviation elements**. This might include stylized aircraft silhouettes, map-inspired textures, or subtle references to classic aviation instruments, all rendered in a clean, modern style. The goal is to feel fresh and exciting, not dated.
