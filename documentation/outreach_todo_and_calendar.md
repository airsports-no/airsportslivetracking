You do not need to become a full-time marketer. Protect your coding time by treating outreach as a simple **2-hour-per-week engineering checklist**.

---

## The Weekly Rhythm (The "2 × 60-Minute Rule")

Put two recurring 60-minute blocks on your calendar every week:

* **Tuesday (60 minutes) — New Outreach:**
  1. Pick 3–5 target clubs/coaches from the table below for that week's theme.
  2. Look up their current contact email (club committee page, EAA chapter directory, 99s chapter site, or university flight team page).
  3. Paste the matching template from `documentation/outreach_playbook_and_templates.md`, customize `[Name]` and 1 sentence about their club, and send.
  4. Record the `Date Sent` and `Follow-Up Date (+7 days)` in the tracker below.

* **Thursday (60 minutes) — Follow-Ups & Onboarding:**
  1. Send **Template 5 (The 3-Sentence 7-Day Follow-Up)** to anyone from 7 days prior who hasn't replied.
  2. Reply immediately to anyone who said *"Yes, let's try it"*:
     - Offer a 15-minute screen-share—or take 5 minutes in the Route Editor to draw a sample 25-minute beginner corridor or 4-airport Poker Run around their home airport ICAO code and send them the link.
     - Point them to `https://airsports.no/docs/12_First_Contest_in_15_Minutes` and `https://airsports.no/docs/17_Club_Event_Recipes` so they can set up their club contest for free.
