# Air Sports Live Tracking — Pilot-to-Pilot Outreach Playbook & Templates

This playbook gives you ready-to-paste emails and forum posts written in a **non-commercial, fellow-pilot voice**. You are not pitching SaaS software—you are a Norwegian pilot and engineer maintaining an open-source, non-profit project (backed by European air sports federations) to help keep precision navigation and club flying sports alive.

---

## 1. Golden Rules for Pilot-to-Pilot Outreach

1. **Ban SaaS & sales words:** Never write *"free trial," "complimentary pass," "pricing tier," "solution,"* or *"unlock capacity."* Volunteer chapter presidents delete those immediately. Write like a pilot sending a note from the hangar: *"open-source project," "non-profit," "funded by our national air sports federations," "trying to keep the sport alive."*
2. **State the economics simply and honestly:**
   - The **entire web platform is free** for clubs and organizers (funded by national air sports federations).
   - The iOS/Android mobile app only has an **$8.99/year subscription** (less than two gallons of avgas) to prevent misuse and help keep the tracking servers running, and the **MSFS 2020/2024 client is free**.
   - **Your hands-on offer:** Offer to draw a sample 25-minute beginner route or Poker Run around their home airport (`[Airport ICAO]`)—or hop on a quick 15-minute call—so all they have to do is check it out or share the link.
3. **Ask for their advice instead of pitching (especially for US EAA & AOPA chapters):** Reference their actual home airport (`[Airport ICAO]`) and ask whether a GPS Poker Run or 30-minute corridor game would fit their chapter's fly-outs or monthly **VMC Club** meeting. Pilots love sharing how their club flies.
4. **Keep it short and include the 1-click Live Replay link:** The animated map replay (`https://app.airsports.no/competition-map/774/2734?mode=playback&time=2025-07-06T11:45:50&speed=40&autoplay=true&rankingExpanded=true`) explains the entire concept in 5 seconds.
5. **Follow up once, 7 days later:** Most positive replies arrive after a short, polite 3-sentence bump.
