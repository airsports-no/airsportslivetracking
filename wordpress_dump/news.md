# NEWS

**SERVER STATUS: Normal**

#### JANUARY 2024, GOOGLE CLOUD HOSTING

**Chance of intermittent service interruptions**

During the autumn of 2023 we moved the hosting of https://airsports.no to the Google cloud. Most of the infrastructure is now hosted in the Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), and through the use of their _best effort_ service level agreement (SLA) we have been able to get the hosting cost as low as $4000 a year. Unfortunately this means that our service is subject to interruptions when it is moved to make room for someone of higher priority. While this is annoying, rest assured that it is handled automatically and interrupted flights will be automatically continued within a few minutes when correct operation has been restored. **Results and scoring of an ongoing competition will not be affected while the server is unavailable.** This annoyance can be resolved through increased financial support, but on today’s budget this is the best solution that continues to keep the platform free and available to everyone.

We are in need of donations if we are to keep the service freely available, or change to a reliable service level agreements to avoid the intermittent interruptions. We estimate that a reliable solution costs approximately $7000 a year. Please contact your local flying club or federation to let them know that this is a useful tool that needs to be kept available to all. Details regarding donations can be found on our homepage <https://home.airsports.no/>.

#### JANUARY 2024, AIR SPORTS LIVE TRACKING IS OPEN SOURCE

We are thrilled to announce that we are releasing Air Sports Live Tracking as open source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/airsports-no/airsportslivetracking), and to continue building the best Live Tracking system for competition flying together.

We believe the move to an open-source release has several benefits for all of us:

  * Transparency. The scoring algorithms are available for review and validation.
  * Opening up for community contributions. We believe the platform has huge potential, but to achieve this we need more people working on: 
    * Adding new tasks to support the large variety of flying competitions (GA, microlight, paramotor, ballooning, et cetera) out there.
    * Improving documentation and the help system.
    * Improving the user interface and the usability.
    * Better support for adding sponsors and logos to single competitions.
    * Translations.
  * Persistence. Making the source code publicly available ensures that it remains available.



We encourage you to take a look, join the discussion, and help shape the future of Air Sports Live Tracking – [GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/airsports-no/discussions). We will continue to quality assure all changes and new functionality, to ensure the quality of the Air Sports Live Tracking system.

#### MAPS UPDATE JULY 2023

A Finnish national raster map has been added with scale 1:200,000. Works best with best fit, but also between 1:100-250k with a zoom level 12-13. You get the best resolution on this map with zoom level 13, which you select in the _flight order configuration_. Here you also select _**Finland200K**_ as the standard default map for your competition. Updated July 2023.

A Swedish national raster map has been added with a scale 1:100,000. Works best with best fit, but also between 1:100-250k with a zoom level 12-13. Select **_Sweden100k_** as the standard default map for your competition. Updated July 2023.

A Norwegian map is included at 1:250,000, Updated July 2023.

#### new LIVE scoring INDICATOR (Air Sports ARROW)

We are revolutionizing competiton flying for the spectators, by introducing a live score indicator arrow. Here you can follow the crew towards a time control. The arrow gives you an indication of how the participant is doing and you will immediately see the penalty points live in the results. This makes it easy to follow the excitement down to the LAST second !!  
The danger zone thermometer🌡 warns if the aircraft is on its way out of the corridor or on its way to a danger area. If the crew continues outside, you immediately see the penalty points start to run and the points are updated live.  
This makes Precision Flying, ANR or the new Air Sports Race even more exciting to follow on live TV or on airsports.no
