# Club Event Recipes: 5 Ready-to-Run Formats

You do not need a national jury, ground marshals, or specialized logger boxes to run an exciting flying club competition. These five 1-page "recipes" are built for busy club coordinators, CFIs, and fly-in organizers who want to set up an event in 15 minutes using just pilots' smartphones (or Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020/2024).

> **First time organizing?** Read [How ASLT Works](/docs/00_How_ASLT_Works) and [Your First Contest in 15 Minutes](/docs/12_First_Contest_in_15_Minutes) first.

---

## Recipe 1: The "Zero-Volunteer" Weekend GPS Poker Run

* **Best for:** Saturday fly-ins, pancake breakfasts, EAA chapter fly-outs, and Ninety-Nines scholarship fundraisers.
* **Why pilots love it:** Gives pilots a fun reason to visit 4–5 nearby airfields without needing volunteers sitting at remote tables dealing paper playing cards.
* **Difficulty:** Entry-level (any licensed pilot or student with a CFI).

### 10-Minute Organizer Setup
1. **Become an Organizer:** Log in at `https://app.airsports.no/` and click **Become an Organizer** in the top bar (instant).
2. **Create a Contest:** Go to **My contests → Create New Contest**, set your club's airfield location, and set visibility to **Public** (or keep it **Private** and share with your club [Squadron](/docs/13_Squadrons)).
3. **Draw the Course:** Open **Route editor → Create new route**. Draw **Gate Polygons** over 4–5 destination airfields. Optional: add an "Extra Card Gate" guarded by tighter virtual penalty zones for brave crews.
4. **Create the Task:** Add a **Navigation Task** of type **Pilot Poker Run**, tick **Allow self-management** so pilots can register themselves, and set task visibility to **Public**.
5. **Print & Project:** Click **Generate Hangar Flyer** to print your custom QR registration poster for the clubhouse wall, and open the **Live Map** on your clubhouse TV with `?display=screen` ([Airfield Screens](/docs/15_Live_Map#airfield-screens)).

* **Pilot experience:** Scan the QR flyer -> enter tail number -> press **Start Tracking** in the ASLT mobile app before taxi -> fly over the target airfields -> walk back into the hangar to see your live track, penalties, and dealt poker hand on the big screen.
* **Full details:** [Quick Start: Poker Run](/docs/09_Quick_Start_Poker_Run)

## Recipe 2: The "Corridor & Burgers" Evening (Beginner-Friendly ANR)

* **Best for:** Weekday club evenings or Saturday mornings; pairing a student or low-time pilot with an experienced navigator.
* **Why pilots love it:** Fast, visual, and zero intimidating calculations. Crews simply try to keep the airplane inside a GPS corridor on their chart and watch the animated replay together over burgers.
* **Difficulty:** Entry-level to intermediate.

### 10-Minute Organizer Setup
1. **Draw a Beginner Loop:** In **Route editor → Create new route**, draw a 25–35 NM loop around local landmarks starting and ending near your home field.
2. **Create the ANR Task:** Add a **Navigation Task** of type **ANR Corridor** and pick the **FAI ANR 2022** scorecard. Set a generous beginner corridor width (e.g., **0.4–0.5 NM** with **Rounded Corners** enabled) so first-timers stay motivated instead of racking up huge penalties.
3. **Enable Flexible Launches:** Tick **Allow self-management** so crews can register themselves and tick **Adaptive Start** when booking—letting each plane cross the start gate whenever ready within a 2-hour window.
4. **Debrief Together:** Put the **Live Map** (`?display=screen`) on the clubhouse monitor and use **Replay** mode after landing so crews can compare where they drifted out of the corridor.

* **Pro tip:** As club crews get sharper over the season, narrow the corridor from 0.5 NM down to 0.3 NM or add a couple of prohibited penalty zones.
* **Full details:** [Route Creation and Tasks](/docs/04_Route_Creation_and_Tasks) and [The Live Map](/docs/15_Live_Map)

## Recipe 3: The Season-Long "Home Airfield Challenge" (Club Strava)

* **Best for:** Flight schools, CFIs, and clubs looking to boost mid-week and Sunday aircraft utilization.
* **Why pilots love it:** A permanent 25–30 minute navigation course right outside the home airfield that any member can fly solo or dual whenever an airplane is free—and see where they sit on the monthly club leaderboard.
* **Difficulty:** All levels.

### 10-Minute Organizer Setup
1. **Build One Permanent Route:** Draw one 25–30 NM local route in **Route editor** with 4–6 clear visual checkpoints. Because routes are reusable blueprints, you only draw this once.
2. **Create Monthly Rounds:** Create a contest for each month (or bi-weekly round) and attach your route. Turn on **Allow self-management** and email notifications under **Update task details → E-mail the contest managers** so you automatically receive each pilot's score when they land.
3. **Connect a Season Cup (Series):** Open **Series** in the navigation bar, create *"Your Club Name Cup 2026"*, and link each monthly contest as a leg. Enable *"only the best N legs count"* so pilots who miss a month can still win the cup.
4. **Repeat Effortlessly:** Use **Manage → Duplicate…** on a task to spin up next month's round in 30 seconds.

* **Full details:** [Series: Cups and Championships](/docs/14_Series) and [Register for a Navigation Task](/docs/16_Register_for_a_Navigation_Task)

## Recipe 4: No-Marshal Time-Gate (SEMA / CIMA) & Spot-Landing Practice

* **Best for:** Building real airmanship, dead-reckoning discipline, time-on-target precision, and spot-landing skills—and preparing rookies for regional or national precision/microlight rallies.
* **Why pilots love it:** Tests precision timing down to a 1-second window and ground observation without needing volunteers standing in fields with stopwatches.
* **Difficulty:** Intermediate to advanced.

### 10-Minute Organizer Setup
1. **Use the Task Wizard:** Open **Route editor**, click **Task Wizard**, and select either a **Precision** route (with SP, TP, FP, hidden secret gates, and optional photo observation markers) or a **CIMA** task type such as **2.A2 Precision navigation**, **2.A1 Curve navigation with time estimation**, or **2.A7 Circle**.
2. **Add Optional Spot Landing:** Add a **Landing** task inside the same contest to combine navigation airmanship with landing accuracy.
3. **Automatic Flight Orders:** Once pilots register (or are scheduled with the **Flight Scheduler Optimizer**), ASLT automatically emails each crew a printable **Flight Order PDF** containing the route map, turnpoint photos, and observation photos.
4. **Automated Scoring:** Hidden timing gates, speed-keeping, and track deviations are scored live from the mobile app's GPS telemetry—no manual spreadsheet entry required.

* **Full details:** [CIMA and Legacy Task Guide](/docs/11_CIMA_Task_Guide) and [Contest Manager Guide](/docs/03_Contest_Manager_Guide)

## Recipe 5: Rainy-Day / Winter Clubhouse MSFS 2020 & 2024 Night

* **Best for:** Winter months, IFR/low-ceiling weekends, and evening hangar socials when real aircraft are grounded.
* **Why pilots love it:** Zero fuel cost, zero weather cancellations, and pilots learn the exact ASLT registration, flight-order, timing, and corridor workflow before flying it for real in spring.
* **Difficulty:** All levels (great way to get students and non-owners involved).

### 10-Minute Organizer Setup
1. **Reuse Any Real Route:** Take your club's real local ANR, Precision, or Poker Run route—or build a fun destination challenge in the **Route editor**.
2. **Enable Simulator Flying:** In task settings, make sure simulator tracks are allowed (or run a dedicated virtual club contest).
3. **Install the MSFS Client:** Pilots download the free **Airsports for MSFS** bridge from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N4MZBKPDS5X?hl=en-us&gl=NO&ocid=pdpshare) (works with both MSFS 2020 and MSFS 2024).
4. **Fly Together:** Put the **Live Map** on the clubhouse projector (or share via Discord/Slack for pilots flying from home) and watch everyone fly the exact same corridor simultaneously.

* **Full details:** [MSFS Integration: Virtual Competition](/docs/08_MSFS_Integration)

## Want Help Setting Up Your Club's First Event?

The entire platform is completely **free to use** for flying clubs and organizers (funded by European air sports federations), with only a small **$8.99/year subscription** on the iOS and Android tracking apps to prevent misuse and help cover telemetry servers (flying in Microsoft Flight Simulator is completely free).

If your flying club, university flight team, EAA/99s chapter, or national association wants help drawing your first local route or getting started, email **support@airsports.no** or join our [Slack Community](https://join.slack.com/t/airsportslivetracking/shared_invite/zt-2mmaui668-tEaJvJgoqg7782m3bdTleg).
