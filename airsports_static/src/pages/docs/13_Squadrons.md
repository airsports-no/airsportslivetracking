# Squadrons: Sharing Private Contests with a Group

A **squadron** is a group of people: a flying club, a national team, a training group, or just the friends you fly with. You share a private contest with the squadron once, and everybody in it can see the contest and take part in it.

---

## When should I use a squadron?

Use one when **a contest should not be open to the whole world, but more than a handful of people need to be in**. Typical cases:

| Situation | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| A club championship that only the club members should see and fly in | Make a squadron of the club members, keep the contest **private**, share it with the squadron. |
| Training camp or team selection for a national team | One squadron for the team, share each training contest with it. |
| Several organizers run events for the same group of pilots | Everybody who manages a contest can share it with the same squadron. |
| An open event anybody can watch | You do not need a squadron. Make the contest **public**. |
| One person needs to look at your contest | Give that person access in the *Contest access* window of the contest, no squadron needed. |

## What a squadron member can do

When a contest is shared with a squadron, every member can:

- **see** the contest, its tasks (also the private ones), the live map and the results, and find it on their dashboard,
- **take part**: register a team, book a start time and fly.

Members **cannot manage** the contest. They cannot edit it, change tasks, run the scheduler or share it further. Managing a contest is still given to people one by one in *Contest access*.

Being in a squadron does not give anybody access to anything else of yours, and it does not make anything public.

## Roles in a squadron

| Role | Can do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Member** | See and take part in the squadron's contests, leave the squadron, take away the contests they added themselves. |
| **Admin** | Everything a member can, plus invite and remove people, make others admin, edit the name and description, make an invite link, and take away any contest. |

The person who creates the squadron is its first admin. A squadron always has at least one admin.

## Step by step

### 1. Create the squadron

1. Log in and open **Squadrons** in the navigation bar.
2. Under *Start a squadron* give it a name people will recognize, and a short description of who it is for. Press **Create**.

Any logged-in user can create a squadron, you do not need to be an organizer.

### 2. Get people in

Open the squadron and use one of:

- **Invite by name or e-mail.** Search for somebody who already has an account, or type an e-mail address. They get an e-mail and see the invitation under **Squadrons** when they log in. They join when they press **Join**.
- **Invite link.** Press *Create an invite link* and post the link in your group chat. Anybody who is logged in and opens it can join as a member. Anyone with the link can use it, so switch it off or make a new link when it has been shared too widely.

### 3. Share a contest with the squadron

There are two ways, and the result is the same:

- From the **contest**: open the contest, then *Contest access → Squadrons*, choose the squadron and press **Share**.
- From the **squadron**: under *Contests* choose one of the contests you manage and press **Add**.

You can only share contests you manage. Any member of a squadron who manages a contest may share it with that squadron. The same contest can be shared with several squadrons; people who are in more than one see it once.

### 4. Stop sharing

Use **Stop sharing** on the contest, or **Remove** on the squadron's contest list. You can always take away a contest you manage or one you added. A squadron admin can take away any contest from the squadron. The members lose access immediately, unless the contest is public or they have been given access in another way.

## Good to know

- **Making the contest public makes the squadron unnecessary.** Everybody can see a public contest. The squadron is for contests that stay private.
- **Private tasks are visible too.** People with access to a private contest see all its tasks. If you need to hide a task from the pilots until a briefing (for example at a championship), do not share the contest with the squadron until then, or keep the task in a contest that is not shared.
- **Someone who leaves a squadron** loses access to the contests that came through it. A member can leave at any time from the squadron page.
- **Deleting an account** hands the squadron over to its longest-standing member if the person was its only admin.
- **Privacy:** members see each other's names, but e-mail addresses only in a masked form.

## Questions

**Pilots say they cannot see my contest.** Check that the contest is shared with the squadron (*Contest access → Squadrons*), and that the pilot has accepted the invitation and is listed under the squadron's members.

**Can a squadron be used instead of a club?** They are different things. A club is a pilot's affiliation shown on results. A squadron only decides who may open a private contest.

**Can I see which contests I can open through my squadrons?** Yes. The **Squadrons** page lists them with the squadron that gives you access.
